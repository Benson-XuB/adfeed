FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need GTINs / barcodes?

No. If you don’t have real barcodes, AdFeed uses the compliant no-ID path. We never invent fake GTINs.

Will this guarantee Merchant Center approval?

No. We clean feed quality and reduce common 1688 import risks. Final approval is always Google’s decision.

Do I upload a new file every time I regenerate?

Usually no. AdFeed hosts a stable feed URL. After you regenerate, Google can fetch the same URL on schedule.

What does “products × markets” mean?

Each generate counts one selected SKU in one selected market. Example: 1 SKU for US, UK, and CA = 3. That is our billing unit under the hood.
