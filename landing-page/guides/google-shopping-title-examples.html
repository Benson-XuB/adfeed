Google Shopping Title Examples: Weak vs Clearer

Short answer: Shopping titles should read like a product someone would click— not a supplier dump or a uniqueness keyword wall. Color and size for this offer once is enough.

What “good enough” looks like

Aim for a short product skeleton shoppers understand: who it’s for, what it is, a useful material or style cue, then this row’s color and size—not every variant and promo phrase in one string.

Weak vs clearer examples

Weak (hard to scan)Clearer (Shopping-card friendly)

SKU-88921 Skirt High Waist Floral Print Style 1 Brown Style 2 Women's High-Waist Midi Skirt Floral S

Keep color as a real color. Pattern / “Style 1” belongs in pattern (or out of the title)— not jammed into color for uniqueness.

Common title mistakes

  • Promo noise — “hot sale,” “free shipping,” “new arrival” don’t help the shopping card.
  • Factory / SKU dumps — codes and supplier strings belong in ops fields, not the title shoppers see.
  • Attribute walls — every size and color in one title is harder to read than one clear offer.
  • Invented “fixes” — don’t pad with fake brand names or fake identifiers to look complete.
