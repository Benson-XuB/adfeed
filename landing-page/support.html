FAQ

Do I need a separate AdFeed account?

No. Install from Shopify Admin. The app uses your Shopify session—no extra username/password.

How do I put the feed into Google Merchant Center?

Copy the persistent feed URL from AdFeed, then in Merchant Center add a product data source that fetches from a URL (scheduled fetch). Paste the URL and save. Full steps: How it works.

Why is my feed “Limited” or pending?

New feeds often sit in Google’s review queue for days. AdFeed does not guarantee approval. Fix brand, images, and attributes in the app, regenerate, and let Google fetch again.

Does AdFeed invent barcodes?

No. We never invent GTIN or COGS. Use the no-identifier path when you lack real barcodes.
